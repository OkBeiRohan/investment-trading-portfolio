## 📖 Overview

Different sectors operate with unique business models, regulatory environments, and performance drivers. This module provides deep-dive analysis into 8 major Indian sectors, teaching you sector-specific metrics, industry cycles, and key performance indicators (KPIs) that matter beyond standard financial ratios.

You'll learn why embedded value matters for insurance companies (not just P/E), how CASA ratio drives bank profitability, why utilization rates are critical for steel/cement, and how ADR (Average Daily Rate) affects hotel valuations. Each sector chapter explains the value chain, competitive dynamics, regulatory landscape, and how to compare companies within that sector.

This knowledge is essential for sector rotation strategies, identifying cyclical opportunities, and avoiding value traps (companies with low P/E but structurally declining industries). By the end, you'll be able to analyze any company through a sector-specific lens, not just generic financial metrics.

## 🔑 Key Concepts

### Sector Classification

| Category               | Sectors                     | Characteristics                                  |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Cyclical**           | Auto, Steel, Cement, Hotels | Revenue tied to economic growth, high volatility |
| **Defensive**          | FMCG, Pharma, Utilities     | Stable demand, low volatility                    |
| **Growth**             | IT, Financials, Telecom     | Structural demand drivers, high growth           |
| **Interest-Sensitive** | Banking, Real Estate, NBFCs | Profitability affected by interest rates         |

### Key Metrics by Sector

#### Banking

```
NIM (Net Interest Margin) = (Interest Income - Interest Expense) / Avg Earning Assets
Benchmark: 3-4% (higher = better)

CASA Ratio = (Current + Savings Accounts) / Total Deposits
Benchmark: >40% (low-cost deposits)

Gross NPA = Gross NPAs / Gross Advances
Benchmark: <3% (asset quality)

Example (HDFC Bank):
- NIM: 4.2% (industry-leading)
- CASA: 48% (sticky deposits)
- GNPA: 1.3% (clean book)
```

#### Insurance

```
VNB (Value of New Business) = PV of future profits from new policies
Margin: VNB / APE (higher = profitable product mix)

Embedded Value = Net worth + PV of future profits from existing policies
P/EV Ratio = Market Cap / Embedded Value (valuation metric)

Example (HDFC Life):
- VNB Margin: 26% (high-value ULIP/protection mix)
- P/EV: 3.5x (premium valuation vs 2x industry avg)
```

#### IT Services

```
Revenue Mix:
- Banking: 35%, Retail: 20%, Manufacturing: 15%, etc.
- Diversification reduces client concentration risk

Attrition Rate = Employees left / Avg headcount
Benchmark: 12-18% (lower = stable talent pool)

EBIT Margin = EBIT / Revenue
Benchmark: 20-25% (TCS, Infosys maintain ~24%)
```

#### Cement

```
Utilization Rate = Production / Installed Capacity
Benchmark: >70% for profitability (fixed cost leverage)

Realization per Tonne = Revenue / Tonnes sold
Tracks pricing power (affected by demand, competition)

Cost per Tonne = Total Cost / Tonnes produced
Lower cost → higher margins (driven by fuel, freight, power)
```

#### Hotels

```
ADR (Average Daily Rate) = Room Revenue / Rooms Sold
Metric: Pricing power (₹5,000 vs ₹8,000 ADR)

Occupancy Rate = Rooms Sold / Available Room Nights
Benchmark: 65-75% for profitability

RevPAR = ADR × Occupancy Rate
Key Metric: ₹5,000 × 70% = ₹3,500 RevPAR
```

## 💡 Practical Applications

### For Investors

**Sector Rotation Strategy (Economic Cycle):**

| Phase           | Economic Condition      | Outperforming Sectors          | Underperforming Sectors       |
| --------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| **Expansion**   | GDP growth accelerating | IT, Auto, Banking, Cement      | Defensive (Pharma, Utilities) |
| **Peak**        | GDP growth peak         | Commodities (Steel, Metals)    | Early cyclicals               |
| **Contraction** | GDP growth slowing      | Pharma, FMCG, Utilities        | Cyclicals (Auto, Real Estate) |
| **Trough**      | GDP growth bottoming    | Financials (distressed assets) | Capital goods                 |

**Example (2020-2022 Recovery):**

- 2020 (Trough): Overweight Pharma, IT (defensive + remote work)
- 2021 (Expansion): Rotate to Financials, Auto (pent-up demand)
- 2022 (Peak): Commodities (Steel, Cement) benefit from infra push

**Stock Screening (Banking Sector):**

```
Filters:
- CASA Ratio >40%
- GNPA <3%
- CAR >14% (well-capitalized)
- RoA >1.5%

Result: HDFC Bank, Kotak Mahindra, Axis Bank (shortlist for deep-dive)
```

### For Traders

**Event-Driven Trading (Sector-Specific):**

- **Auto Sales Data (Monthly):** Trade Maruti, M&M, Tata Motors based on volume growth surprises
- **Monsoon Forecast:** Cement demand tied to rural construction (good monsoon → buy cement stocks)
- **RBI Policy:** Rate cuts → buy banking stocks (NIM expansion expected)
- **Crude Oil Prices:** OMCs (Oil Marketing Companies) - inventory gains/losses

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

1. **Using same valuation metric across sectors:** P/E for banks ≠ P/E for IT (different capital intensity)
2. **Ignoring regulatory changes:** RBI norms on NPA recognition changed banking landscape (2015-2018)
3. **Not tracking capacity utilization:** Low utilization = poor pricing power (cement, steel)
4. **Buying cyclicals at peak P/E:** Auto at ₹50 P/E (peak demand) vs ₹15 P/E (trough)
5. **Comparing asset-light vs asset-heavy:** Hotel management contracts (high margin) vs owned hotels (high capex)
6. **Ignoring working capital:** Auto dealers (high inventory) vs IT (negative working capital)
7. **Not understanding value chain:** Crude up → OMCs squeezed (regulated retail prices)
8. **Overlooking substitution risk:** EVs disrupting ICE auto, digital wallets eating bank transaction fees
9. **Focusing only on top-line:** Insurance (high premium growth but low VNB margin = unprofitable mix)
10. **Not reading sectoral reports:** Missing industry-specific trends (RBI FSR, IRDAI annual report)
