## 13.1 – Dig deeper

Congratulations on completing Module 1 - Introduction to Stock Markets! You now have a foundational understanding of how stock markets work.

But this is just the beginning. To truly succeed in markets, you need to dig deeper.

Zerodha Varsity offers comprehensive modules covering:

1. **Technical Analysis** – Chart patterns, indicators, candlesticks
2. **Fundamental Analysis** – Financial statements, valuation, company analysis
3. **Futures Trading** – Understanding derivatives, leverage, hedging
4. **Options Theory** – Call/Put options, Greeks, volatility
5. **Option Strategies** – Spreads, straddles, combinations
6. **Markets & Taxation** – Tax implications of trading/investing
7. **Currency, Commodity & Government Securities** – Alternative markets
8. **Risk Management & Trading Psychology** – Position sizing, discipline
9. **Trading Systems** – Pair trading, momentum strategies
10. **Personal Finance (Mutual Funds)** – MF selection, portfolio building
11. **Integrated Financial Modelling** – Building financial models

## 13.2 – So many modules, how are they interrelated?

Think of learning stock markets like learning to drive:

**Module 1 (Introduction)** = Understanding what a car is, its parts, traffic rules

**Module 2 (Technical Analysis)** = Learning to read road signs and predict traffic flow

**Module 3 (Fundamental Analysis)** = Understanding the engine, maintenance, fuel efficiency

**Modules 4-6 (Derivatives)** = Advanced driving techniques like racing, drifting

**Modules 7-9** = Understanding road regulations, safety measures, and mental preparation

**Modules 10-11** = Planning road trips, managing expenses

The key insight: **Markets reward those who understand risk management above all.**

You can have the best stock picking ability, but without risk management, you'll eventually blow up your account. Conversely, with proper risk management, even mediocre stock picking can make you wealthy over time.

## 13.5 – Beyond Varsity

Once you complete Varsity, consider:

1. **Paper Trading** – Practice without real money
2. **Start Small** – Begin with small amounts
3. **Track Trades** – Maintain a trading journal
4. **Continuous Learning** – Markets evolve, keep learning
5. **Community** – Join trading communities, learn from others

## 13.6 – Final Words

Stock market success is not about:

- Getting rich quick
- Finding "hot tips"
- Following TV experts blindly
- Copying others' trades

Stock market success IS about:

- Patient learning
- Disciplined execution
- Risk management
- Continuous improvement
- Long-term thinking

The journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step. You've taken that step by completing this module. Now keep moving forward!
