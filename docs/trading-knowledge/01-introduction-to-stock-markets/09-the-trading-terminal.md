## 9.1 – Overview

A trading terminal is the software interface through which you buy and sell stocks. In India, Zerodha's Kite is one of the most popular trading platforms.

## 9.2 – Market Watch

The market watch displays real-time prices of stocks you're tracking. Key elements:

- **LTP (Last Traded Price)** – Latest price at which stock traded
- **Change** – Price change from previous close
- **% Change** – Percentage change
- **Volume** – Number of shares traded
- **Bid/Ask** – Buy/sell prices

## 9.3 – Stock Quote

Clicking on a stock shows detailed information:

- Open, High, Low, Close prices
- Previous Close
- Total Buy/Sell Quantity
- Average Price
- Circuit Limits

## 9.4 – Placing an Order

To place an order:

1. Click on stock in market watch
2. Choose Buy (B) or Sell (S)
3. Select order type:
   - **Market** – Executes at current price
   - **Limit** – Executes only at your specified price
4. Enter quantity
5. Choose product type:
   - **CNC** – Delivery (shares go to demat)
   - **MIS** – Intraday (must square off same day)
6. Review and submit

## 9.5 – Market Depth

Market depth shows top 5 buy and sell orders:

**Buy Orders (Bids)**

- Price
- Quantity
- Number of orders

**Sell Orders (Asks)**

- Price
- Quantity
- Number of orders

This helps understand demand-supply dynamics.

## 9.6 – Order Types Explained

**Market Order**

- Executes immediately
- No price guarantee
- Use when you want to enter/exit quickly

**Limit Order**

- Executes only at specified price or better
- May not execute if price doesn't reach your limit
- Use when you want specific entry/exit price

**Stop-Loss Order**

- Triggers when price hits your stop level
- Converts to market/limit order
- Use to limit losses

## 9.7 – Conclusion

Mastering the trading terminal is crucial. Practice with small amounts before trading larger sums. Understand all features before going live.

---

## Key Takeaways

1. Trading terminal is your gateway to markets
2. Market watch shows real-time prices
3. Market depth reveals demand-supply
4. Different order types serve different purposes
5. Always review order before submitting
6. Practice on paper before using real money
