## 🔑 Key Concepts

### SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India)

- Primary regulator for stock markets
- Protects investor interests
- Ensures fair trading practices

### Stock Exchanges

- **BSE (Bombay Stock Exchange)**: Oldest exchange in Asia (1875)
- **NSE (National Stock Exchange)**: Largest by volume (1992)

### Indices

- **Sensex**: 30 stocks, BSE benchmark
- **Nifty 50**: 50 stocks, NSE benchmark
- **Calculation**: Free-float market capitalization weighted

### Order Types

- **Market Order**: Execute immediately at best available price
- **Limit Order**: Execute only at specified price or better
- **Stop-Loss Order**: Trigger when price reaches specified level

### Corporate Actions

- **Dividend**: Cash payout to shareholders (ex-date adjustment)
- **Stock Split**: Increase shares, reduce price (e.g., 1:2 split)
- **Bonus Issue**: Free shares (e.g., 1:1 bonus)
- **Buyback**: Company repurchases shares (supports price)

## 📊 Quick Reference

### Important Terms

- **ISIN**: International Securities Identification Number
- **Demat**: Dematerialized (electronic shares)
- **T+2**: Settlement in 2 days after trade
- **Free Float**: Shares available for public trading
- **Market Cap**: Share price × total shares

### Key Regulators

- **SEBI**: Stock market regulator
- **RBI**: Banking, currency, monetary policy
- **IRDAI**: Insurance regulator
- **PFRDA**: Pension fund regulator

### Trading Hours (NSE/BSE)

- **Pre-open**: 9:00 AM - 9:15 AM
- **Normal**: 9:15 AM - 3:30 PM
- **Post-close**: 3:40 PM - 4:00 PM
