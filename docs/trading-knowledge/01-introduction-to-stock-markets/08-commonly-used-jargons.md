# Commonly Used Jargons

> **Essential stock market terminology every investor should know**

**Module:** Introduction to Stock Markets

## Trading Terms

**Bull Market** – Market with rising prices and positive sentiment

**Bear Market** – Market with falling prices and negative sentiment

**Long Position** – Buying a stock expecting price to rise

**Short Position** – Selling a stock (that you don't own) expecting price to fall

**Square Off** – Closing an existing position

**Market Order** – Order executed immediately at current market price

**Limit Order** – Order executed only at specified price or better

**Stop Loss** – Order to limit losses by auto-selling if price falls below specified level

## Stock-Related Terms

**Face Value** – Original value assigned to a share (usually Rs. 1, 2, 5, or 10)

**Market Price** – Current trading price of a share

**52-Week High/Low** – Highest/lowest price in last 52 weeks

**Volume** – Number of shares traded

**Market Cap** – Total value of company (Shares × Price)

**Bid Price** – Price at which buyers are willing to buy

**Ask Price** – Price at which sellers are willing to sell

**Spread** – Difference between bid and ask price

## Circuit Breakers

**Upper Circuit** – Maximum price a stock can rise in a day (usually 5%, 10%, or 20%)

**Lower Circuit** – Maximum price a stock can fall in a day

Circuits prevent extreme volatility and panic selling/buying.

## Corporate Actions

**Dividend** – Profit distributed to shareholders

**Bonus Shares** – Free shares given to existing shareholders

**Stock Split** – Dividing each share into multiple shares (e.g., 1:2 split means 1 share becomes 2)

**Rights Issue** – Offering new shares to existing shareholders at discounted price

**Buyback** – Company buying back its own shares from market

## Order Types

**CNC (Cash and Carry)** – Delivery-based order (shares go to demat)

**MIS (Margin Intraday Square-off)** – Intraday order (must sell same day)

**BO (Bracket Order)** – Advanced order with target and stop-loss

**CO (Cover Order)** – Intraday order with compulsory stop-loss

## Portfolio Terms

**Diversification** – Spreading investments across different stocks/sectors

**Asset Allocation** – Distributing money across different asset classes

**Liquidity** – Ease of buying/selling without affecting price

**Volatility** – Measure of price fluctuations

## Analysis Types

**Fundamental Analysis** – Analyzing company's financials, business model, management

**Technical Analysis** – Analyzing price charts and patterns

**Quantitative Analysis** – Using mathematical models and statistics

---

## Key Takeaways

1. Understanding jargons is essential for effective trading
2. Bull market = rising, Bear market = falling
3. Long = buy, Short = sell first
4. Circuit breakers prevent extreme volatility
5. Corporate actions affect share price and quantity
6. Different order types serve different purposes
